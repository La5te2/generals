Terrain icons

The mountain, obstacle, city and crown artwork is by generals.io.
Website: https://generals.io/
Original files:
https://generals.io/mountain.png
https://generals.io/obstacle.png
https://generals.io/city.png
https://generals.io/crown.png

src/interface/icons.inc contains simplified triangle meshes adapted from
these images. The project author reported permission from the site
administrator to use the artwork with attribution on 2026-10-05.
This attribution accompanies source and binary distributions.
The artwork retains its separate permission terms and generals.io credit.
